# gcloud transcoder — command index

manage Transcoder jobs and job templates

- `gcloud transcoder jobs create` — create Transcoder jobs
- `gcloud transcoder jobs delete` — delete transcoder jobs
- `gcloud transcoder jobs describe` — describe transcoder jobs
- `gcloud transcoder jobs list` — list transcoder jobs
- `gcloud transcoder templates create` — create Transcoder job templates
- `gcloud transcoder templates delete` — delete transcoder job templates
- `gcloud transcoder templates describe` — describe transcoder job templates
- `gcloud transcoder templates list` — list transcoder job templates
